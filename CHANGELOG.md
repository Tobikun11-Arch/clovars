## [0.1.0] - YYYY-MM-DD

First public release.

### Added
- Sign up and log in
- Workspaces with three roles: Owner, Admin, and Member
- Invite team members by email or invite code
- Clients, projects, tasks, and tags
- Time tracking with a timer, including start/stop from the system tray
- Manual time entry
- Reports by project, member, or date, with CSV export
- Server-side permission checks for all protected routes
